About

BOYUE INDUSTRY

A premium full-package brand
from China's Greater Bay Area,
delivering to Singapore & Malaysia.

BOYUE INDUSTRY is based in Foshan, Guangdong, serving Singapore and Malaysia with one-stop space design, construction and full-package delivery. Centered on premium interior design and whole-house custom millwork, we leverage China's mature home-improvement supply chain to serve overseas Chinese homes, villas, showrooms and offices with integrated solutions.

  • 02

    Stainless-steel cabinets, millwork, wardrobes and kitchens — shipped by container.

  • 03

    Eco Materials

    Direct sourcing of certified eco-friendly home-improvement materials from China.

  • 04

    Local Craftsmanship

    Local crews for renovation, anti-mould treatment, and on-site installation.
